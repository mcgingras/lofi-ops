# Weapons

Guns, attachments, equipment (grenades), the knife, viewmodels, third-person models, sounds and the sniper scope. The numbers are scaled from Black Ops 1 to 100 health.

## What's in

Each weapon has: damage falloff, hit-part multipliers (with an optional `lowerTorso`), rpm, fire mode, magazine and reserve, reload times (plus `shellReload` timings for shotguns), ADS time, sprint-out time, move speed, spread, recoil, unlock level, price and blurb. Use `LOADOUT_WEAPONS` for create-a-class lists. `shotsToKill`, `timeForRounds`, `ttkMs` and `cycleSec` give stat bars and bots the same maths the server uses.

**Penetration** (`src/shared/penetration.ts`, rules in docs/CONTRACTS.md section 13): a human's bullets go through thin walls, a bot's never do. Each weapon has a type (`penetration`, default by class as in BO1): pistols and SMGs `small` (15 cm of wood), ARs, the Python and the Desert Eagle `medium` (30 cm), snipers and the LMGs `large` (45 cm), as BO1's weapon files have them, shotgun pellets `pellet` (4 cm), the launcher and the knife `none`. Drywall and sheet metal count half (an SMG goes through a 25 cm drywall partition or 20 cm of corrugated sheet), concrete and brick ten times (nothing gets through a concrete wall), rock, steel and bullet-stopping glass never. At most two walls; each keeps x0.75 (paper thin) to x0.5 (at the limit) of the damage, and halves the range left. An AK through a Nuketown house wall (25 cm siding) does x0.54. Hardened doubles the depth (BO1's `perk_bulletPenetrationMultiplier` 2); Hardened Pro's challenge is 10 kills through walls (BO1 counts penetration kills only). Feedback: a puff where the bullet goes in and where it comes out (everyone sees both; the killcam too), a bracket around the hitmarker for a hit through a wall, and a small wall icon in the killfeed for a penetration kill.

**Attachments** (`attachments.ts`): red dot, reflex, ACOG (zooms differently on snipers and drops the scope overlay there), variable zoom (snipers: 4.5x / 7x / 10x), suppressor (keeps you off the radar, range x0.7), extended mag, dual mag (reloads x0.72), grip (recoil x0.6), grenade launcher, rapid fire (SMGs), dual wield (pistols: double mag and rate, no ADS), speed reloader (Python). Each weapon's `attachments` list sets what fits it. On top of that you get one per slot (optic, muzzle, underbarrel, magazine, other) and **one in total** (BO1 rules). `validAttachments(def, ids, max)` and `canAttach(def, id, others, max)` are the shared rule. Every attachment has `unlockKills` (kills with that weapon) and `price`.

**Equipment** (`equipment.ts`): Frag (cook it by holding G, it bounces, a 3.5 s fuse, and it blows up in your hand if you hold it too long), Semtex (sticks to surfaces and players), Tomahawk (BO1: level 18; kills on contact, bounces off walls and floors so bank shots count, and lies where it stops for 60 s for anyone carrying tomahawks to walk over, or USE within 2 m, to get it back; kills are `weapon: 'tomahawk'`, `means: 'thrown'`), Flashbang (blinds and deafens anyone who can see it, more when they face it; teammates are spared), Concussion (slows movement x0.55 at full strength, and slows ADS). You carry 1 lethal and 2 tacticals. **Every** lethal and tactical is raised when you press the button, stays up while you hold it, and is thrown when you let go (BO1); only the frag burns its fuse in your hand.

### Lethal grenades at BO1 strength

BO1's own frag weapon file is not in any public dump, so the numbers come from the Call of Duty Wiki's BO1 infoboxes (MediaWiki API: "Frag Grenade" damage 200-75, radius 6.5 m; "Semtex" 200-55, the frag's radius), cross-checked with Treyarch's weapon files either side of BO1: World at War's `frag_grenade_mp` (explosionRadius 256, inner 300, outer 10, fuseTime 3.5, cookOffHold 1) and Black Ops II's `frag_grenade_mp` (256, 200 / 75, fuse 3.5) and `sticky_grenade_mp` (256, 185 / 50, fuse 2, cookOffHold 0). The engine rules are BO1's (KisakBlack decompile, `game_mp/g_combat_mp.cpp`):

- **Falloff**: `G_RadiusDamage` interpolates linearly, `(inner - outer) * (1 - d / radius) + outer`.
- **Distance**: players are measured to their origin, i.e. their feet (`G_GetRadiusDamageDistanceSquared`; only brush models use their bounds).
- **Cover**: `CanDamage` traces from the blast to five points: the middle of the body (halfway from the feet to the eye) and four corners 15 units to either side, square to the blast, at eye and foot level. The damage is multiplied by hits / 3, capped at 1: three clear traces take the full blast, two 2/3, one 1/3, none nothing. A wall stops a blast; a post or a crate hiding part of you takes some off; fences and foliage (`bullets: false`) take nothing. We add one guard: a corner only counts when the middle of the body sees it, so a thin wall you stand against never lets a blast round it. A grenade stuck to you always reaches you.
- **Flak Jacket**: `perk_flakJacket` 35 (`_class.gsc` `cac_modified_damage`: "explosive damage is this % of original"), so x0.35, and never for a sticky grenade stuck to the victim. That makes a Flak Jacket wearer at full health survive one frag at their feet.
- **Semtex**: BO1 has no scripted auto-kill, but a stuck semtex sits at the centre of its blast, always reaches its host and skips their Flak Jacket, so it always kills. Here it passes `ensureKill`, which also beats armour (the Infected First Infected's x0.5) and any extra health.
- **Self and team**: your own frag hurts you (core TDM, as in BO1); teammates are never hurt (friendly fire off).
- **Grenade indicator**: BO1's `cg_hudGrenadeIconMaxRangeFrag` is 250 units (6.35 m) in the T5 dvar defaults.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frag (BO1) | 6.5 m (256 u) | 200 - 75 | 3.5 s, cookable | 5.2 m from your feet | never on its own (70 at most) |

Not changed: the launcher round (`GL_ROUND`, 5 m, 150 - 40), tomahawk, tacticals.

**Bots and grenades** (`src/server/features/bots/grenades.ts`, used by the brain): after a fight, at the spot they last saw an enemy, 0.6-3 s after losing sight of them, never closer than the lethal's kill radius + 3 m (so never in their own blast) nor further than 22 m, never with a teammate within 6 m of the spot. Each chance is taken 8% + 22% x aggression of the time (checked every 1.5 s), then a cooldown of 22 + 0-8 s - 6 s x aggression (16-30 s; one lethal per life anyway). They aim on the low arc that drops the first bounce short of the spot (`grenadePitch`, the server's throw maths), keep facing it through the wind-up (they used to look away before the release), and the spot itself is off by up to 2.5 m + 0.5 m per degree of aim error (regular 5.75 m, veteran 3.1 m), so a regular's frag at a spot you stand still on misses the kill about a fifth of the time. Recruits don't throw lethals (BO1's Combat Training: `sv_botAllowGrenades` 0 on Easy, 1 on Normal / Hard / Fu). In 4-minute bot-only TDM sims (10 bots) they throw 0.2-1 lethals per bot-minute and grenades make 0-7% of kills (before: 0-4%).

**Cook timer** (frag, any `cookable` equipment): while the pin is out a bar under the crosshair drains to the detonation with the seconds left (a notch per second), a dry tick plays once a second (`weapons.cook`, synthesized), and the last second flashes red with "THROW" and ticks four times a second. The server owns the fuse: it pulls the pin when it applies the command carrying the press and blows the grenade up in the hand once `cookLeft(fuse, held)` reaches 0 (shared maths in `shared/defs/equipment.ts`: `COOK`, `cookLeft`, `cookWarning`, `cookTickTimes`, `cookTicksBetween`). It acks the pin with `weapons.cook {e, seq, fuse}`, `seq` being that command; the client maps the seq back to the local time of the frame that sent it (`client/features/weapons/cook.ts`) and runs the timer from there. That is the deadline a release has to beat on the same input timeline, so a release sent while the HUD still shows time reaches the server before the fuse (exact with steady latency; a burst of late commands only makes the HUD early, never late). The timer starts predicted on the press, is dropped if no ack comes within a round trip, and follows the server if it lets go first (`weapons.throw` for yourself). Keyboard (hold G) and controller (hold RB) take the same path. Controller: RB / LB, the same held-button path. The knife (V/E) lunges up to 3.2 m inside a 32° cone and kills in one hit. It has a 0.9 s cooldown, and the gun stays lowered for 0.75 s after a swing.

- **Intervention vs L96A1**: the same kill profile on purpose (the wiki and the weapon file agree on MW2's 70 x1.5). The Intervention cycles its bolt in 0.92 s (L96: 1.46 s) and reloads a partial mag faster, and its 300 ms sprint-out means a quickscope out of a sprint lands at the same 400 ms as the L96 (ADS runs during sprint-out). Standing still it scopes in 50 ms slower, and it kicks harder, sways more, holds its breath for less time and carries 15 spare rounds instead of 20.
- **ACR**: a long-range laser. Four shots out to 46 m (the AK-47 is three to 30 m, so it loses up close), almost no kick, quick reloads.
- **M16A4 vs M16**: the BO1 `m16` stays as it was. The M16A4 holds its one-burst kill further (38 m against 30 m) but drops to 30 damage sooner (51 m against 55 m), waits longer between bursts (264 ms against 200 ms: 459 rpm against 549 rpm overall), has a faster empty reload (2.36 s against 2.63 s), a slower sprint-out (300 against 250 ms), moves at x0.9, carries 60 spare rounds instead of 120 and has no Dual Mag. The model is a flat-top: no carry handle, a full-length top rail with a flip-up rear sight (folded under an optic, which sits on the rail), a square quad-rail handguard and the long A2 stock. It also has its own name, voice (pitch 0.98, darker EQ) and unlock (level 40).
- **Attachments** (BO1 set, MW2 lists): Holographic Sight becomes the Reflex Sight; Red Dot, ACOG, Silencer (Suppressor), Extended Mags and the M203 (Grenade Launcher) map directly; the Intervention's Thermal Scope becomes the Variable Zoom. MW2's FMJ, Shotgun and Heartbeat Sensor have no equivalent here and are left out; Dual Mag and Grip never existed on these MW2 guns. Extended Mags is the usual x1.5 (MW2 doubled the Intervention to 10; here it's 8).
- **Models** (`gunkit.ts`): Intervention: fluted barrel (dark ribs in the flutes), big muzzle brake, round free-float handguard with a folded bipod, long rail with an oversized scope, skeleton stock with cheek riser and monopod, bolt on the right (the L96 bolt animation). ACR: chamfered upper, faceted tan handguard, forward charging handle, flip-up sights, angular folding stock, polymer mag. All three build through the shared kit, so third-person, dropped guns, the killcam viewmodel and the Create-a-Class preview follow automatically.
- **Elsewhere**: bots pick them through the class weights; Sharpshooter rotates them by class; the Gun Game ladder lists the M16A4 after the M16, the ACR after the AUG and the Intervention before the L96A1 (20 shipped rungs, as in BO1). Weapon challenges, attachment unlocks and store items are generated as for every gun.
- **Sounds**: `WEAPON_SHOTS` entries (Intervention on the sniper pool, pitch 0.95; ACR and M16A4 on the AR pool, pitch 1.03 / 0.98, each with its own EQ) and synth fallbacks (`VOICES` in `sounds.ts`). No new audio files.

### Wonder weapons (Mystery Box only)

The seven BO1 Zombies wonder weapons, handed out only by the Mystery Box killstreak (on a 60 s loan) (docs/features/perks-streaks.md) and, once dropped, by the weapon pickups feature. They are `wonder: true` defs in `weapons.ts` (class `special`, slot `primary`), with their behaviour and tuning in `src/shared/defs/wonder.ts` and the server in `src/server/features/weapons/wonder/`. Never in create-a-class (`LOADOUT_WEAPONS` and `sanitizeLoadout` drop them), the store (`storeCatalog`, `enforceLoadout`), unlocks (`isUnlocked` is false, not in `unlockTable`), bot classes (class `special` has no weight) or Sharpshooter (not in its classes). Kills count like any gun's (`weapon.<id>.kills`, the generated `w_<id>_kills` challenges, killstreak progress).

Sources: Call of Duty Wiki pages (Ray Gun, Thundergun, Wunderwaffe DG-2, Winter's Howl, Scavenger, 31-79 JGb215, Wave Gun, MediaWiki API) and the T5 script dump (plutoniummod/t5-scripts `ZM/Common/maps/_zombiemode_weap_thundergun.gsc`, `_zombiemode_weap_tesla.gsc`, `_zombiemode_weap_freezegun.gsc`, `_zombiemode_weap_shrink_ray.gsc`, `_zombiemode_weap_microwavegun.gsc`). Tuning lives in plain tables (`RAYGUN`, `THUNDERGUN`, `WUNDERWAFFE`, `WINTERS_HOWL`, `SCAVENGER`, `BABY_GUN`, `WAVE_GUN`), so a generic upgrade can scale their damage or the defs' magazines. BO1 numbers are for zombies with thousands of health; ours are for 100-health players: strong, fun, never a kill from across the map.

How it works:
- **Server** (`wonder/`): the seven `registerWeaponBehavior`s (`raygun.ts`, `thundergun.ts`, `wunderwaffe.ts`, `wintershowl.ts`, `scavenger.ts`, `babygun.ts`, `wavegun.ts`; instant beams share `lineStrike` in `common.ts`), registered from the weapons feature's `setup` and `room`. Each pull also emits the core `fire` room event (the killcam records the shot, progression counts it, hardware on the line takes it; a Wunderwaffe hop is its own `fire` from the previous victim) and pushes a `weapons.wonderFx` effect (`splash`, `blast`, `arc` per hop, `frost`, `shatter`, `fling`, `boom`, `shrink`, `kick`, `zap`, `wave`, `pop`) to everyone. Per room (`wireWonder`): bolts fly each tick (`updateBolts`: swept ray against the world and bodies), frost thaws and its `weapons.frost` entity follows the player (`s.p` id, `s.f` frost), `creditEdgeKills` gives a launched player's fall out of the map to the shooter (looking a few ticks ahead so core's out-of-bounds kill never gets there first), and the frost modifiers (`speedMultiplier`, `canSprint`, `fireRateMultiplier`, `spreadMultiplier`, `canFire`, `canAds`, `botFrozen`, `botSkillMultiplier` / `botSkillOffset`) take the fight out of a frosted player so prediction follows. Scavenger bolts fly, ride and go off (`updateScavBolts`, entity `weapons.scavbolt`: `s.k` stuck, `s.p` carrier, `s.f` blast time); shrunk players carry `weapons.shrunk` (`s.p`, `s.u` grow-back time; `wireShrink`: legs, damage dealt, the last-word `beforeDamage` that makes any hit on the tiny body lethal, kicks in `updateShrunk`); swelling players carry `weapons.swell` (`s.p`, `s.t` pop time; `wireWave`, `updateSwell`). The weapons feature's knife and throws respect `canFire` too.
- **Knockback**: the Thundergun's launch goes through the shared movement (`applyKnock`, `MoveState.knock`, docs/features/movement.md), so the victim's own client predicts the flight from the snapshot: only the first snapshot carrying the launch corrects it.
- **Client** (`src/client/features/weapons/wonder/`): `models.ts` (the four guns in the kit style, added to `GUNS` so first person, third person, dropped guns, the killcam viewmodel and the box's cycle all get them: the Ray Gun's brick-red body with glowing green tubes and flared chrome emitter, the Thundergun's turbine of fan discs (they turn a notch each blast) and copper flare, the Wunderwaffe's glowing bulbs (they drop out on a reload like BO1's tubes) and twin prongs, the Winter's Howl's frosted flaring nozzle and coolant canister, the Scavenger's long olive rifle with a fat scope, cycling bolt and finned launcher muzzle with a red-tipped bolt, the Baby Gun's stubby cream-and-copper body with a pink glass chamber and ringed dish, the Wave Gun's two blue Zap pistols that snap together side by side as you aim (`GunBuild.animate`)), `fx.ts` (effects for pushes, the local pull and killcam replays; the bolt and frost entities; shattered bodies vanish and Thundergun kills are thrown back), `fx-more.ts` (the Scavenger's blinking, beeping bolt and big blast, the pink shrink beam, kicks, zap bolts, the rippling Wave Gun beam, swelling and pops), `bodies.ts` (what happens to a remote soldier as drawn: an icy tint while frosted, tiny while shrunk, swollen and flushed while swelling), `hud.ts` (frost creeping in from the screen edges, "FROZEN", the look slowed by `frostSlow(f).turn` (frozen: none); "STUCK!" with a red pulse, "SHRUNK" with its countdown and a pink edge (your shots squeak), a red heat edge while swelling; killfeed icons), `sounds.ts` (synthesized: `shot.raygun` sci-fi zap, `shot.thundergun` air cannon, `shot.wunderwaffe` electric discharge, `shot.wintershowl` icy hiss, `shot.scavenger` crack and thunk, `shot.babygun` toy warble, `shot.wavegun` electric snap; `weapons.wonder.splash/zap/shatter/chill/wave/pop/scavboom/squeak/kick`; all listed in `SFX_SYNTH_ONLY`, no audio downloads).
- **The local pull** (`registerShotFx(id).local`, called by `Match` on the trigger pull): a predicted Ray Gun bolt (the server's copy of your own bolt shows after a moment), the blast / frost from the muzzle at once, and the first Wunderwaffe arc to whatever is on the line, so you never wait a round trip; the server's copies of those are skipped for the shooter. All seven have `projectile: true` (no local hitscan tracer). The client's trigger also holds while `me.params.canFire` is false (frozen solid).
- **Killcam** (`registerShotFx(id).replay`, called by the per-death killcam for the killer's recorded shots): the bolt flies and bursts, the blast rolls, arcs chain (a shot within 0.6 s and a hop's range of the last one starts from its end), the frost sprays.
- **Pickups**: they drop on death like any gun (except while their Mystery Box loan runs: the owner keeps it, `keepsWeapon`) and can be swapped for; a dropped one despawns after 20 s (`WONDER_DROP_LIFE_SEC`; others 45 s). A bot with full guns still detours for one within ~10-20 m (by its `weaponPickup` skill).
- **Bots** (`wonder/bots.ts`, consulted by the brain): Ray Gun: mid range (16 m), ADS past 10 m, fires out to 45 m, its bolt leads a moving target for them; Thundergun: rushes, fires only inside 11 m, past 16 m switches to the other gun if it has ammo (and back up close); Wunderwaffe: 18 m, ADS past 12 m, fires out to 42 m; Winter's Howl: closes in like a shotgun, fires inside 18 m, switches past 24 m; Scavenger: 30 m, always scoped, fires out to 66 m, switches to the other gun inside 8 m (its own blast), its bolt leads and lifts for them; Baby Gun: 12 m, fires inside 28 m, switches past 32 m, and every bot aims at a shrunk enemy's tiny body (the brain's aim point); Wave Gun: zaps from the hip at 14 m, aims (the Wave Gun) past 20 m, fires out to 48 m. Frozen or swelling bots don't fire (`botFrozen`).

## How it works

Server (`src/server/features/weapons/`):
- `beforeSpawn` (priority -10, after progression's unlock gate) trims the primary to `1 + attachmentSlotsOffset(p, loadout)` attachments in the order picked (Warlord: 2) and the secondary to one; the modifier gets the loadout being spawned because `p.loadout` is still the previous life's. `spawn` hands out the equipment (`count + equipmentCountOffset(p, kind)`, Warlord Pro +1 each; pushing `weapons.equip` with the Scout `breath` and Steady Aim Pro `melee` multipliers) and, for a primary with `gl`, adds weapon slot 2 (`gl`). `perks.resupply` (Scavenger Pro) refills grenades up to that cap. The knife's cooldown and recovery scale with `meleeSpeedMultiplier`.
- Button edges call `melee()` on MELEE and `startThrow()` on LETHAL/TACTICAL (the pin; the count drops here). `updateThrow` keeps it in the hand while the button is held and releases it on the release edge, no sooner than `THROW_WINDUP` after the press; a cookable frag explodes in hand past its fuse. While the knife recovers or a throw is in progress (held or flying out), the server sets `p.swapEnd`, so core refuses to fire ("switching"). Dying with a frag up drops it with its fuse; dying with a tomahawk up drops it as a pickup.
- Tomahawks are `weapons.tomahawk` entities (`tomahawk.ts`): `stepBody` with more bounce, a kill on any player the swept path touches while faster than 5 m/s (each player once per throw, the thrower never), then at rest `s.k = 1` and the pickup check (walk within 1 m, or hold USE within 2 m, while carrying fewer than your loadout count). `weapons.retrieve {e, n}` goes to the picker, `weapons.retrieved` on the bus.
- `startThrow` on a cookable grenade sends `weapons.cook {e, seq, fuse}` to the thrower (`seq` = `p.lastSeq`, the command being applied); `updateThrow` and the release use `cookLeft` for the fuse.
- Projectiles are room entities of kind `weapons.nade` (`s.e` = equipment id or `gl`, `s.k` = 1 when stuck). They are simulated each tick by `projectile.ts` (gravity, swept raycast, bounce, friction, sticking; the stepping itself, `stepBody` / `throwVelocity`, lives in `shared/projectile.ts` so the client can predict throws: the Care Package marker uses it). Explosions go through `room.radiusDamage` (BO1's rules, `shared/blast.ts`; self damage on; a stuck semtex passes `ensureKill`). Then `weapons.boom` is broadcast; a semtex stuck to a player carries `s.p` (their id) for the indicator.
- The GL round has to fly 8 m to arm. Before that, a direct hit does 60 impact damage and anything else is a dud.
- Shotgun shell reloads: the `reload` event retimes `reloadEnd` to open + shells + close (+ pump when the tube was empty). Shells go in one at a time, and holding FIRE cancels the reload once at least one shell is in.
- Core changes: burst cadence and the lower-torso multiplier now live in core `fire()`.

Client (`src/client/features/weapons/`):
- `gunkit.ts`: procedural low-poly builders for every model id, plus optics (open tubes, so sight pictures stay visible), suppressor, grip, launcher, extended and dual mags, and dual-wield pistols. First-person and third-person models are built from the same code.
- `viewmodels.ts`: hands and sleeves, knife and grenade actions, and per-family animation: magazine swap, L96 bolt cycle (after each shot and around reloads), pump rack, shell feeding, Olympia break-action, revolver cylinder, pistol slide.
- `worldmodels.ts`: puts the weapon model in each remote soldier's hands (hiding `Soldier.gun`) and moves the muzzle to match. Remotes swing their arms when they knife or throw.
- `sounds.ts`: a synthesized voice per weapon (`shot.<id>`, `shot.<id>.sup`), plus bolt, pump, shell, mag, knife, pin, bounce, explosion, flashbang and ring sounds. These are the fallbacks: the recorded versions (`WEAPON_SHOTS` and `SFX_BINDINGS` in `src/shared/defs/sfx-recipes.ts`, files in `assets/sfx/`) take over once loaded. Guns share class recordings (FFSL near mics and Freesound previews, with their natural tails) and are told apart by their variant pools, gentle EQ and gain (playback rate within +-6%); suppressed guns use the suppressed recordings.
- `scope.ts`: the pixel scope overlay (it replaces `core.scope`), sway (hold Shift to steady it, with a breath meter and an out-of-breath penalty), variable zoom (X or middle mouse while scoped), and local mechanical sounds, kept sparse: one mag out / mag in at 20% / 68% of the reload (rifle recordings, or `weapons.magout.pistol` / `weapons.magin.pistol` for pistols), the charging handle (`weapons.charge`) at 86% of an AR/SMG/LMG reload that started empty, Olympia break open / close (`weapons.break` / `weapons.breakclose`), and bolt and pump after the shot. No brass tinkle after shots. (Core no longer plays a sound when a reload starts.)
- `cook.ts` (pure): the frame history (`pushFrame`, `frameTimeOfSeq`) that anchors the cook timer on the server's ack. `equipment.ts` draws the timer (`weapons.cook` HUD widget, order 27) and plays the ticks.
- `equipment.ts`: grenade and tomahawk models (the tomahawk spins end over end in flight and lies flat once retrievable), blast glow, puffs and shake, the flashbang whiteout (a Bayer-dithered fade drawn as HUD pixels), concussion wobble and slower look, the equipment counter (bottom right, with a hatchet icon), the grenade indicator, and the local hold / release viewmodel actions (`ready:<id>` held on the press, `release:<id>` on release).
- **Explosion feedback** (`blastJolt` in `equipment.ts`, `blastFeel` / `SHAKE` in `camfx.ts`): every blast that rattles you goes through `blastJolt(m, shake, ring)`: camera jitter (0.6 s, up to 4.5 cm / 0.045 rad), controller rumble and, close in, ringing ears (`weapons.ring`). A lethal or launcher blast shakes at full strength inside its kill radius, fading out by 18 m, and rings your ears inside about 1.6 kill radii. Killstreak blasts (Napalm, Mortar, Rolling Thunder, Valkyrie, RC-XD, the Care Package trap) shake out to 30 m and ring inside their blast; the Thundergun shakes along its tube; the Nuke's shockwave shake is scaled the same way. Flak Jacket Pro ("Shockproof") turns all of it off (`equipState.shock`, from the server's `blastShakeMultiplier` in `weapons.equip.shock`).
- **Grenade indicator** (`danger.ts`, pure; drawn by the `weapons.danger` widget): BO1's icon on a ring around the crosshair (`cg_hudGrenadeIcon*`) with a pointer aimed at the grenade (top = ahead, bottom = behind), for every live frag or semtex that can hurt you (an enemy's or your own; teammates' never, friendly fire being off) within its kill radius plus `GRENADE_INDICATOR.margin` and `maxHeight` above or below your eye. Red and solid inside the kill radius, yellow and blinking in the margin; at most four, nearest first. A semtex stuck to you always shows (the server tags the entity `s.p` with the player it stuck to).
- `viewmodels.ts` `actionHands(c, gunRoot, hip)`: the knife hand and the throwing hand (grenade or hatchet) shared by every viewmodel, including the economy's crossbow and ballistic knife. It undoes the rig's raise dip while an action plays.

Controls: 1/2 switch weapons, **3** switches to the underbarrel launcher (or use the wheel), V/E knife, G lethal (hold to raise, release to throw; a frag cooks while held), Q tactical (same), Shift while scoped to hold breath, X or middle mouse to change zoom.

HUD ammo hint (core, `src/client/hud/ammo-hint.ts`, pure): "PRESS R TO RELOAD" only when a reload can load something (reserve > 0 and the mag isn't full) and the mag is at a quarter or less; "NO AMMO" when mag and reserve are both empty; "LOW AMMO" (no prompt, no flashing) when the mag is low and the reserve is empty on a magazine weapon. Single-load weapons (crossbow, ballistic knife, Olympia) never nag. The server's `startReload` refuses with no reserve, so pressing R does nothing (no `reload` event, no animation or sound). Weapons with `ammoNoun` show "N BOLTS" / "N BLADES" (loaded + spare) with a pip per round.

## Integration notes

- **perks-streaks**: Warlord is `room.modifiers.add('attachmentSlotsOffset', (p, loadout) => loadout.perks.includes('warlord') ? 1 : undefined, 'perks.warlord')` and Warlord Pro `equipmentCountOffset` (+1). Tactical Mask is `tacticalMultiplier` (0.35), Steady Aim Pro `meleeSpeedMultiplier`, Scout `holdBreathMultiplier`. Scavenger Pro emits `perks.resupply`, which refills up to the carry cap. Flak Jacket should check `info.means === 'explosive'` with `weapon` in `frag | semtex | gl` in `damageTaken`. Sleight of Hand goes through `reloadMultiplier`, which shell reloads respect.
- **progression / economy**: use `LOADOUT_WEAPONS` with `unlockLevel` and `price`, and `ATTACHMENTS` with `unlockKills` (per-weapon kills) and `price`. Attachment challenges can listen to `death` (`weapon` is the base weapon id, `knife`, `frag`, `semtex` or `gl`) and `weapons.tactical`. The AK-47 stays at level 1 because core's `DEFAULT_LOADOUTS` use it.
- **frontend**: the create-a-class builder should filter with `LOADOUT_WEAPONS` and `canAttach(def, id, current, 1 + extraSlots)`, and use `LETHALS` / `TACTICALS` for the equipment pickers. Weapon and attachment `blurb` give you menu text, and `shotsToKill` / `ttkMs` give you stat bars. Key rebinding: `KEYMAP.Digit3 = 'weapon3'`.
- **bots**: a bot shoots with `room.fire`. Burst, bolt and pump cadence is enforced on the server, so a bot can just fire every tick. It knifes by pressing `Buttons.MELEE` or calling `melee(room, bot)`, and throws with `Buttons.LETHAL` / `TACTICAL` or `startThrow(room, bot, 'lethal')`. Holding LETHAL cooks a frag. Your brain can read `flashedAmount(room, bot)` / `stunnedAmount(room, bot)` (0..1) to blind or slow the bot, and `meleeTarget(room, bot)` to decide when to knife. All of these are exported from `src/server/features/weapons/index.ts`. To use the launcher, set `weaponSlot: 2` when `bot.weapons.length === 3`. Grenade entities (`room.entities.byKind('weapons.nade')`) are there for danger avoidance.
- **pickups**: dead players drop the gun in hand and anyone can swap for it (docs/features/pickups.md). The dropped gun uses `GUNS` / `Kit` / `FURNITURE` from `src/client/features/weapons/gunkit.ts` with its attachments and camo; a swap rebuilds the `gl` slot (slot 2) to follow the gun that carries the launcher.
- **modes / economy wagers**: grenade counts are rebuilt at every spawn from `player.loadout.lethal/tactical`. Set those in `beforeSpawn` (e.g. `null` for Sticks and Stones variants). Remote weapons render without attachments, because snapshots only carry the weapon id.
- Core files touched (all small): `server/combat.ts`, `shared/ballistics.ts`, `shared/loadout.ts` (default classes now have grenades), `client/game/{match,controls,viewmodel,audio}.ts`, `client/render/models/{viewmodels,soldier}.ts`, `docs/CONTRACTS.md`.
